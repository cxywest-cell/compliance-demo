Step 5b · Create Counter-Transfer (RFI) 🔒 Reject a transfer to unlock
Create RFI Transfer with Your Requirements

POST /entity/:did/tx — Since EA cannot query your policies (403 Forbidden), create a new transfer (RFI) with your own REQUIRE_PRESENTATION policy. EA will see this as an incoming RFI with actionRequired: true and can read exactly which fields you require.

FA-1000 = FATF baseline Travel Rule (full originator identity: name, DOB, nationalId, address). This communicates your actual requirements to EA.
Review Counter-Transfer

After creating the counter-transfer, refresh here to check if EA has appended PII. When the policy status shows COMPLETED, EA has fulfilled your requirements.
