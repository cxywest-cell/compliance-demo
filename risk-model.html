Foundation — Customer Classification
◇
CRS Classification → Investor Category
Determines Customer Risk base score & verification requirements
▶

All customers are corporate entities. The CRS Entity Self-Certification form is the first gate — it determines investor category, which sets the Customer Risk base and what additional verification is needed.

Institutional Investor
CRS: Financial Institution / Investment Entity
✓ KYB: license + registration
✓ Entity AML screening
✕ SoW: NOT required
✕ SoF: NOT required
✓ UBO structure (controlling persons)
Base Risk: LOW (1–2)
Qualified Investor
CRS: Active NFE or Passive NFE
✓ KYB: incorporation + good standing
✓ Entity AML screening
✓ SoW: REQUIRED
✓ SoF: REQUIRED
✓ UBO structure + deeper screening
✓ Financial threshold proof
Base Risk: MEDIUM (3–4)
⚠ Passive NFE Special Case
If CRS = Passive NFE, ALL controlling persons (UBOs) must be individually screened. A single UBO sanctions/PEP hit elevates the entire customer to High Risk regardless of entity-level screening.
