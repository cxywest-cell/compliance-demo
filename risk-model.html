Weighted Scoring Model

The combined transaction risk score is a weighted average of four independent factor scores. Each factor is independently scored 0–10, then multiplied by its weight and summed.

Combined Score = (Customer × 0.30) + (Counterparty × 0.25) + (Transaction × 0.20) + (Blockchain × 0.25)

Weights are configurable and should reflect your institution's risk appetite. The defaults above are illustrative.
