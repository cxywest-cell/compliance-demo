All customers are corporate entities. The CRS Entity Self-Certification form is the first classification gate — it determines the investor category, which in turn determines the Customer Risk base score and what additional verification is required.

→

→ Qualified Investor path
✓ KYB: verify incorporation, good standing
✓ Entity screening (Sumsub AML)
✓ Source of Wealth: REQUIRED (documentary proof)

✓ Financial threshold proof (income / net worth)
✓ UBO structure: required + deeper screening
→ Customer Risk base: MEDIUM (3–4)
Passive NFE special case: If CRS classification is Passive NFE, all controlling persons (UBOs) must be individually screened against sanctions / PEP / crime lists. A single UBO hit elevates the entire customer to High Risk regardless of entity-level screening.

Weighted Scoring Model

The combined transaction risk score is a weighted average of six independent factor scores. Each factor is independently scored 0–10, then multiplied by its weight and summed. Red flag overrides (Step 1) bypass this scoring entirely.

Combined = (Customer × 0.25) + (Counterparty × 0.20) + (Geographic × 0.15) + (Transaction × 0.15) + (Blockchain × 0.20) + (Asset/Product × 0.05)

Weights are configurable and should reflect your institution's risk appetite and regulatory requirements (VARA, FATF).
